# Function Words: Prepositions of Place (in, auf, an) - A1

Prepositions of place (*lokale Präpositionen*) answer the question **"Wo?"** (where?) and tell you where something is located or where someone is going. The three most important prepositions for A1 are **in**, **auf**, and **an**.

## Why this matters
- You use them constantly: "I'm **in** Berlin," "The book is **on** the table," "The picture is **on** the wall"
- They are essential for giving and understanding directions
- They change case (accusative or dative) depending on whether there's movement
- Without them, you can't describe where things are or where you're going

## The Golden Rule: Location (Wo?) vs. Direction (Wohin?)

These prepositions can take **two different cases** depending on the situation:

| Question | Meaning | Case | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wo?** (Where?) | Location (no movement) | **Dative** | Ich bin **in dem** Kino. |
| **Wohin?** (Where to?) | Direction (movement towards) | **Accusative** | Ich gehe **in das** Kino. |

**Key insight:** If there's movement from one place to another, use accusative. If you're just describing where something is (static), use dative .

## Part 1: in – in / into

**in** is used for enclosed spaces, buildings, rooms, countries, and cities .

### Location (Wo? – Dative)

| Meaning | Example | Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **in + dem** (masculine/neuter) = **im** | Das Buch ist **im** Regal. | The book is in the shelf. |
| **in + der** (feminine) | Sie ist **in der** Schule. | She is in the school. |
| **in + den** (plural) | Die Kinder sind **in den** Zimmern. | The children are in the rooms. |

More examples:
- Ich wohne **in Berlin**. (I live in Berlin – city)
- Er ist **im** Kino. (He's in the cinema – **in + dem** = **im**)
- Das Essen ist **in der** Küche. (The food is in the kitchen)
- Wir sind **in der** Schweiz. (We are in Switzerland – country)

### Direction (Wohin? – Accusative)

| Meaning | Example | Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **in + das** (neuter) = **ins** | Ich gehe **ins** Kino. | I'm going into the cinema. |
| **in + den** (masculine) | Er geht **in den** Park. | He's going into the park. |
| **in + die** (feminine/plural) | Sie geht **in die** Schule. | She's going into the school. |

More examples:
- Ich fahre **in die** Schweiz. (I'm traveling to Switzerland)
- Wir gehen **ins** Restaurant. (We're going into the restaurant – **in + das** = **ins**)
- Leg das Buch **in den** Schrank! (Put the book into the cupboard!)
- Komm **in die** Küche! (Come into the kitchen!)

### Summary: in

| Case | Masculine | Neuter | Feminine | Plural |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dative (Wo?)** | **im** (in dem) | **im** (in dem) | **in der** | **in den** |
| **Accusative (Wohin?)** | **in den** | **ins** (in das) | **in die** | **in die** |

---

## Part 2: auf – on / onto (horizontal surfaces)

**auf** is used for horizontal surfaces (tables, floors, streets) and also for public events/places (market, concert) .

### Location (Wo? – Dative)

| Meaning | Example | Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **auf + dem** (masculine/neuter) | Das Buch liegt **auf dem** Tisch. | The book is on the table. |
| **auf + der** (feminine) | Die Lampe steht **auf der** Kommode. | The lamp is on the dresser. |
| **auf + den** (plural) | Die Bilder sind **auf den** Tischen. | The pictures are on the tables. |

More examples:
- Das Kind spielt **auf der** Straße. (The child is playing on the street)
- Ich bin **auf dem** Markt. (I'm at the market – literally "on the market")
- Die Tasse steht **auf dem** Tisch. (The cup is on the table)
- Wir sind **auf der** Party. (We're at the party)

### Direction (Wohin? – Accusative)

| Meaning | Example | Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **auf + den** (masculine) | Ich lege das Buch **auf den** Tisch. | I put the book onto the table. |
| **auf + das** (neuter) = **aufs** | Stell die Tasse **aufs** Regal! | Put the cup onto the shelf! |
| **auf + die** (feminine/plural) | Wir gehen **auf die** Party. | We're going to the party. |

More examples:
- Leg dich **auf die** Couch! (Lie down on the couch!)
- Ich stelle die Blumen **auf den** Balkon. (I'm putting the flowers on the balcony)
- Wir fahren **aufs** Land. (We're going to the countryside – **auf + das** = **aufs**)
- Komm **auf die** Bühne! (Come onto the stage!)

### Summary: auf

| Case | Masculine | Neuter | Feminine | Plural |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dative (Wo?)** | **auf dem** | **auf dem** | **auf der** | **auf den** |
| **Accusative (Wohin?)** | **auf den** | **aufs** (auf das) | **auf die** | **auf die** |

---

## Part 3: an – at / on (vertical surfaces) / next to

**an** is used for vertical surfaces (walls, doors), for something being next to something, and for specific locations (the counter, the table – sitting at a table) .

### Location (Wo? – Dative)

| Meaning | Example | Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **an + dem** (masculine/neuter) = **am** | Das Bild hängt **an der** Wand. | The picture is on the wall. |
| **an + der** (feminine) | Er sitzt **am** Tisch. | He's sitting at the table. (**an + dem** = **am**) |
| **an + den** (plural) | Die Jacken hängen **an den** Haken. | The jackets are on the hooks. |

More examples:
- Ich warte **an der** Bushaltestelle. (I'm waiting at the bus stop)
- Wir wohnen **am** See. (We live by the lake – **an + dem** = **am**)
- Das Kino ist **an der** Ecke. (The cinema is on the corner)
- Die Kinder spielen **am** Strand. (The children are playing at the beach)

### Direction (Wohin? – Accusative)

| Meaning | Example | Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **an + den** (masculine) | Ich hänge das Bild **an die** Wand. | I hang the picture on the wall. |
| **an + das** (neuter) = **ans** | Setz dich **ans** Fenster! | Sit down by the window! |
| **an + die** (feminine/plural) | Wir gehen **an den** Strand. | We're going to the beach. |

More examples:
- Komm **an den** Tisch! (Come to the table!)
- Ich stelle mich **ans** Fenster. (I'll stand by the window – **an + das** = **ans**)
- Fährst du **an die** Ostsee? (Are you going to the Baltic Sea?)
- Leg das Buch **an die** Seite! (Put the book aside – literally "to the side")

### Summary: an

| Case | Masculine | Neuter | Feminine | Plural |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dative (Wo?)** | **am** (an dem) | **am** (an dem) | **an der** | **an den** |
| **Accusative (Wohin?)** | **an den** | **ans** (an das) | **an die** | **an die** |

---

## Part 4: Comparison Table – in, auf, an

| Preposition | Use Case | Location (Wo? – Dative) | Direction (Wohin? – Accusative) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **in** | enclosed spaces, buildings, countries | **im** / **in der** / **in den** | **ins** / **in den** / **in die** |
| **auf** | horizontal surfaces, public places | **auf dem** / **auf der** / **auf den** | **aufs** / **auf den** / **auf die** |
| **an** | vertical surfaces, next to, specific spots | **am** / **an der** / **an den** | **ans** / **an den** / **an die** |

## Part 5: Common Contractions

These contractions are used constantly in spoken and written German:

| Contraction | Full Form | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **im** | in + dem | Ich bin **im** Kino. |
| **ins** | in + das | Ich gehe **ins** Kino. |
| **am** | an + dem | Das Bild hängt **am** Fenster. |
| **ans** | an + das | Ich setze mich **ans** Fenster. |
| **aufs** | auf + das | Leg das Buch **aufs** Regal. |

## Part 6: Important Fixed Phrases

These common expressions use specific prepositions – learn them as chunks :

| Phrase | Meaning | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **zu Hause** | at home | No preposition – just "zu Hause" |
| **nach Hause** | (to) home | Direction only |
| **am Wochenende** | on the weekend | Always **am** |
| **im Urlaub** | on vacation | Location |
| **in den Urlaub fahren** | to go on vacation | Direction |
| **auf dem Land** | in the countryside | Location |
| **aufs Land fahren** | to go to the countryside | Direction |
| **an der See** | at the seaside | Location |
| **ans Meer fahren** | to go to the sea | Direction |

## Part 7: Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake 1: Using the wrong case ❌
| Wrong | Correct |
| :--- | :--- |
| Ich gehe **im** Kino. (if you mean direction) | Ich gehe **ins** Kino. |
| Ich bin **ins** Kino. (if you mean location) | Ich bin **im** Kino. |

### Mistake 2: Mixing up auf and an ❌
| Wrong | Correct |
| :--- | :--- |
| Das Bild hängt **auf** der Wand. | Das Bild hängt **an** der Wand. (vertical surface) |
| Das Buch liegt **an** dem Tisch. | Das Buch liegt **auf** dem Tisch. (horizontal surface) |

### Mistake 3: Forgetting contractions ❌
| Wrong but understandable | Natural |
| :--- | :--- |
| Ich bin **in dem** Kino. | Ich bin **im** Kino. |
| Ich gehe **in das** Kino. | Ich gehe **ins** Kino. |
| Das Bild hängt **an dem** Fenster. | Das Bild hängt **am** Fenster. |

### Mistake 4: Using in for horizontal surfaces ❌
| Wrong | Correct |
| :--- | :--- |
| Das Buch liegt **in** dem Tisch. | Das Buch liegt **auf** dem Tisch. |

### Mistake 5: Using auf for vertical surfaces ❌
| Wrong | Correct |
| :--- | :--- |
| Das Bild hängt **auf** der Wand. | Das Bild hängt **an** der Wand. |
