# Nouns: Articles (der / die / das) - A1

German nouns always have an article. Unlike English, where we just say "the" for everything, German has three different words for "the" – one for each gender.

## Why this matters
- You cannot speak German correctly without knowing the article of each noun
- The article affects pronouns (er/sie/es), adjective endings, and cases
- It's the foundation for everything else in German grammar
- Even Goethe-Institut A1 exams test this from day one

## The Three Articles

| Article | Gender | Name | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **der** | masculine | *maskulin* | **der** Mann (the man) |
| **die** | feminine | *feminin* | **die** Frau (the woman) |
| **das** | neuter | *neutral* | **das** Kind (the child) |

**Important:** Grammatical gender doesn't always match biological gender. A girl (*das Mädchen*) is neuter, not feminine!

---

## Why This Matters Later

The article you learn now affects everything else:

| Area | Example with **der** | Example with **die** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pronouns** | **der** Mann → **er** | **die** Frau → **sie** |
| **Accusative case** | Ich sehe **den** Mann | Ich sehe **die** Frau |
| **Dative case** | Ich helfe **dem** Mann | Ich helfe **der** Frau |
| **Possessives** | **sein** Hut (his hat) | **ihr** Hut (her hat) |

---

## Quick Examples

| Article | Noun | Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **der** | **der** Tisch | the table |
| **der** | **der** Hund | the dog |
| **der** | **der** Vater | the father |
| **die** | **die** Lampe | the lamp |
| **die** | **die** Katze | the cat |
| **die** | **die** Mutter | the mother |
| **das** | **das** Buch | the book |
| **das** | **das** Haus | the house |
| **das** | **das** Kind | the child |

---

## The Most Important Rule: Learn the Article with the Noun!

Never learn just "Tisch" – always learn "**der** Tisch". 
Never learn just "Haus" – always learn "**das** Haus".

| Wrong way | Right way |
| :--- | :--- |
| Buch = book | **das** Buch = book |
| Lampe = lamp | **die** Lampe = lamp |
| Hund = dog | **der** Hund = dog |
