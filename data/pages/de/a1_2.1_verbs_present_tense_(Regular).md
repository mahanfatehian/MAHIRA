# Verbs: Present Tense (Regular) - A1

The present tense (*Präsens*) is the most important and most frequently used tense in German. It's used to talk about the present, the future, and habitual actions.

## Why this matters
- Most common tense in daily conversation
- Once you learn the pattern, you can conjugate thousands of verbs
- Essential for forming any basic sentence
- Follows a consistent rule (90% of verbs are regular)

## What is a Regular Verb?
Regular verbs (also called "weak verbs") follow a **predictable pattern** when conjugated. The stem stays the same, and you simply add specific endings.

## The Pattern: Stem + Ending

### Step 1: Find the stem
Take the infinitive (the form ending in **-en** or **-n**) and remove the ending.

| Infinitive | Stem |
| :--- | :--- |
| lernen (to learn) | lern- |
| machen (to do/make) | mach- |
| wohnen (to live/reside) | wohn- |
| spielen (to play) | spiel- |
| kommen (to come) | komm- |

### Step 2: Add the correct ending

| Subject | Ending | Example: **lernen** | Example: **machen** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ich** (I) | **-e** | ich lerne | ich mache |
| **du** (you, informal singular) | **-st** | du lernst | du machst |
| **er/sie/es** (he/she/it) | **-t** | er/sie/es lernt | er/sie/es macht |
| **wir** (we) | **-en** | wir lernen | wir machen |
| **ihr** (you, informal plural) | **-t** | ihr lernt | ihr macht |
| **sie/Sie** (they/you formal) | **-en** | sie/Sie lernen | sie/Sie machen |

## Important Pronunciation & Spelling Rules

Sometimes we need to add an extra **-e-** before the ending to make pronunciation easier.

### Rule 1: Verbs ending in **-t, -d, -m, -n**
When the stem ends in **-t, -d, -m, -n**, add **-e-** before the **-st** and **-t** endings for easier pronunciation .

| Infinitive | Stem | du form | er/sie/es/ihr form |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **arbeiten** (to work) | arbeit- | du arbeit**e**st | er arbeit**e**t |
| **finden** (to find) | find- | du find**e**st | er find**e**t |
| **atmen** (to breathe) | atm- | du atm**e**st | er atm**e**t |
| **zeichnen** (to draw) | zeichn- | du zeichn**e**st | er zeichn**e**t |

**Exception:** If the stem ends in **-m** or **-n** and the letter before it is a vowel or **l, r, h** (makes it flow smoothly), you DON'T need the extra -e-:
- du lern**st** (lern- ends with -n, but r makes it flow: no extra -e-)
- du film**st** (film- ends with -m, but l makes it flow: no extra -e-)

### Rule 2: Verbs ending in **-s, -ß, -z**
When the stem ends in **-s, -ß, -z**, the **du** form gets only **-t** instead of **-st** to avoid double-s sounds .

| Infinitive | Stem | du form | Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **reisen** (to travel) | reis- | du reist | (not reisst) |
| **heißen** (to be called) | heiß- | du heißt | (not heißst) |
| **tanzen** (to dance) | tanz- | du tanzt | (not tanzst) |
| **sitze** is ich form, but **sitzen**? Let's check: | sitz- | du sitzt | (not sitzst) |

## Quick Examples

| Subject | Verb | Sentence | Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ich | lernen | Ich **lerne** Deutsch. | I learn German. |
| du | wohnen | Wo **wohnst** du? | Where do you live? |
| er | arbeiten | Er **arbeitet** bei VW. | He works at VW. |
| sie (she) | kommen | Sie **kommt** aus der Schweiz. | She comes from Switzerland. |
| es | regnen | Es **regnet** heute. | It's raining today. |
| wir | spielen | Wir **spielen** Tennis. | We play tennis. |
| ihr | machen | Was **macht** ihr heute? | What are you guys doing today? |
| sie (they) | kaufen | Sie **kaufen** ein Haus. | They are buying a house. |
| Sie (formal) | heißen | Wie **heißen** Sie? | What is your name? (formal) |

## Word Order Reminder
In main clauses, the conjugated verb is always in the **second position** .

- Ich **lerne** jeden Tag Deutsch. (I learn German every day.)
- Jeden Tag **lerne** ich Deutsch. (Every day I learn German.)
