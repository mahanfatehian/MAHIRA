# Function Words: Prepositions of Time (am, um, im) - A1

Prepositions of time (*temporale Präpositionen*) answer the question **"Wann?"** (when?) and tell you when something happens. The three most important time prepositions for A1 are **am**, **um**, and **im**.

## Why this matters
- You use them constantly: "**at** 8 o'clock," "**on** Monday," "**in** January"
- They are essential for making appointments, talking about schedules, and describing routines
- They follow simple, consistent patterns
- Without them, you can't tell people when things happen

## The Golden Rule: Three Prepositions, Three Uses

| Preposition | Use for | Example | Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **um** | specific times (clock times) | **um** 8 Uhr | at 8 o'clock |
| **am** | days of the week and parts of day | **am** Montag | on Monday |
| **im** | months, seasons, years | **im** Januar | in January |

---

## Part 1: um – for Specific Times

**um** is used for **exact clock times** . It answers the question "Um wie viel Uhr?" (At what time?).

| Question | Answer | Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Um wie viel Uhr** kommst du? | **Um** 8 Uhr. | At 8 o'clock. |
| **Um wie viel Uhr** beginnt der Film? | **Um** 20 Uhr. | At 8 p.m. |
| **Um wie viel Uhr** stehst du auf? | **Um** 7 Uhr. | At 7 o'clock. |

### Examples:

| Sentence | Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| Der Kurs beginnt **um** 10 Uhr. | The course starts at 10 o'clock. |
| Ich habe **um** 12 Uhr Mittagspause. | I have lunch break at 12 o'clock. |
| **Um** wie viel Uhr treffen wir uns? | What time are we meeting? |
| Wir gehen **um** 20 Uhr ins Kino. | We're going to the cinema at 8 p.m. |
| **Um** Mitternacht bin ich müde. | At midnight I'm tired. |

**Important:** **um** is ONLY for clock times, not for days or months!

---

## Part 2: am – for Days and Parts of Day

**am** is used for:
- **Days of the week** (Montag, Dienstag, etc.)
- **Parts of the day** (Morgen, Vormittag, Nachmittag, Abend)
- **Weekends** (Wochenende)
- **Specific dates** (with ordinal numbers)

**am** is a contraction of **an + dem** (dative).

### Days of the week:

| German | English | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **am Montag** | on Monday | **Am Montag** habe ich Kurs. |
| **am Dienstag** | on Tuesday | **Am Dienstag** arbeite ich. |
| **am Mittwoch** | on Wednesday | **Am Mittwoch** gehe ich schwimmen. |
| **am Donnerstag** | on Thursday | **Am Donnerstag** kommt mein Freund. |
| **am Freitag** | on Friday | **Am Freitag** feiern wir. |
| **am Samstag** | on Saturday | **Am Samstag** schlafe ich lange. |
| **am Sonntag** | on Sunday | **Am Sonntag** gehe ich in die Kirche. |

### Parts of the day:

| German | English | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **am Morgen** | in the morning | **Am Morgen** trinke ich Kaffee. |
| **am Vormittag** | in the late morning | **Am Vormittag** arbeite ich. |
| **am Mittag** | at noon | **Am Mittag** esse ich. |
| **am Nachmittag** | in the afternoon | **Am Nachmittag** lerne ich. |
| **am Abend** | in the evening | **Am Abend** sehe ich fern. |
| **am Wochenende** | on the weekend | **Am Wochenende** habe ich frei. |

### Special case: Tonight

For "tonight," Germans use **heute Abend** (without am):

| Correct | Wrong |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Heute Abend** gehe ich ins Kino. | ❌ **Am Abend** gehe ich ins Kino. (means "in the evening" in general) |

**am Abend** = in the evening (generally)
**heute Abend** = tonight (specific)

### Dates (with ordinal numbers):

| German | English | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **am ersten Mai** | on May 1st | Ich habe **am ersten Mai** Geburtstag. |
| **am 15. September** | on September 15th | Wir fahren **am 15. September** in Urlaub. |
| **am 24. Dezember** | on December 24th | **Am 24. Dezember** ist Heiligabend. |

**Note:** When writing dates, you say the ordinal number with -en ending: **am ersten**, **am fünfzehnten**, **am vierundzwanzigsten**.

## Part 3: im – for Months, Seasons, Years

**im** is used for:
- **Months** (Januar, Februar, etc.)
- **Seasons** (Sommer, Herbst, etc.)
- **Years** (2023, 2024, etc.)

**im** is a contraction of **in + dem** (dative).

### Months:

| German | English | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **im Januar** | in January | **Im Januar** schneit es oft. |
| **im Februar** | in February | **Im Februar** habe ich Ferien. |
| **im März** | in March | **Im März** wird es wärmer. |
| **im April** | in April | **Im April** blühen die Blumen. |
| **im Mai** | in May | **Im Mai** ist es schön. |
| **im Juni** | in June | **Im Juni** beginnt der Sommer. |
| **im Juli** | in July | **Im Juli** fahren wir in Urlaub. |
| **im August** | in August | **Im August** ist es heiß. |
| **im September** | in September | **Im September** beginnt die Schule. |
| **im Oktober** | in October | **Im Oktober** ist Erntedankfest. |
| **im November** | in November | **Im November** ist es neblig. |
| **im Dezember** | in December | **Im Dezember** ist Weihnachten. |

### Seasons:

| German | English | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **im Frühling** | in spring | **Im Frühling** blühen die Blumen. |
| **im Sommer** | in summer | **Im Sommer** gehe ich schwimmen. |
| **im Herbst** | in autumn | **Im Herbst** fallen die Blätter. |
| **im Winter** | in winter | **Im Winter** schneit es. |

**Alternative:** **im Frühjahr** is also used for spring.

### Years:

| German | English | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **im Jahr 2023** | in 2023 | **Im Jahr 2023** war ich in Berlin. |
| **im Jahre 2020** | in 2020 (formal) | **Im Jahre 2020** begann die Pandemie. |

In spoken German, you can also just say the year without "im Jahr":
- **2023** war ich in Berlin. (In 2023 I was in Berlin.)

## Part 5: Important Fixed Phrases

These common expressions don't follow the normal rules – learn them as chunks :

| Phrase | Meaning | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **heute** | today | No preposition |
| **morgen** | tomorrow | No preposition |
| **gestern** | yesterday | No preposition |
| **heute Morgen** | this morning | No preposition |
| **heute Abend** | tonight/this evening | No preposition |
| **morgen Früh** | tomorrow morning | No preposition |
| **gestern Abend** | yesterday evening | No preposition |
| **nächste Woche** | next week | No preposition |
| **letzte Woche** | last week | No preposition |
| **diesen Monat** | this month | No preposition |
| **jeden Tag** | every day | No preposition |
| **jedes Wochenende** | every weekend | No preposition |

### Examples:

| Sentence | Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Heute** habe ich keine Zeit. | Today I have no time. |
| **Morgen** fahre ich nach Berlin. | Tomorrow I'm going to Berlin. |
| **Gestern** war ich im Kino. | Yesterday I was at the cinema. |
| **Heute Abend** gehe ich aus. | Tonight I'm going out. |
| **Nächste Woche** habe ich Urlaub. | Next week I have vacation. |
| **Jeden Tag** lerne ich Deutsch. | Every day I learn German. |

## Part 6: Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake 1: Using "in" for days ❌
| Wrong | Correct |
| :--- | :--- |
| **in** Montag | **am** Montag |
| **in** Samstag | **am** Samstag |

### Mistake 2: Using "um" for days/months ❌
| Wrong | Correct |
| :--- | :--- |
| **um** Montag | **am** Montag |
| **um** Januar | **im** Januar |

### Mistake 3: Using "am" for clock times ❌
| Wrong | Correct |
| :--- | :--- |
| **am** 8 Uhr | **um** 8 Uhr |
| **am** 20 Uhr | **um** 20 Uhr |

### Mistake 4: Using "am" with "heute Abend" ❌
| Wrong | Correct |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Am Abend** gehe ich ins Kino. (if you mean tonight) | **Heute Abend** gehe ich ins Kino. |
| **Am Morgen** lerne ich. (if you mean this morning) | **Heute Morgen** lerne ich. |

### Mistake 5: Forgetting that "heute", "morgen", "gestern" need no preposition ❌
| Wrong | Correct |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Am** heute habe ich Zeit. | **Heute** habe ich Zeit. |
| **Im** morgen fahre ich. | **Morgen** fahre ich. |
