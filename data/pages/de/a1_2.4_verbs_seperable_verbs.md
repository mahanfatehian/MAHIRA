# Verbs: Separable Verbs (Trennbare Verben) - A1

Some German verbs consist of two parts: a **prefix** and a **base verb**. These are called **separable verbs** (*trennbare Verben*). When you use them in a sentence, the prefix separates from the base verb and moves to the end [citation:2][citation:6].

## Why this matters
- These verbs are extremely common in everyday German [citation:6]
- They follow a consistent pattern once you understand the rule
- The prefix completely changes the meaning of the base verb
- Mastering them helps you sound more natural in conversation [citation:6]

## The Golden Rule: Prefix Goes to the End!

When you conjugate a separable verb in a **main clause** (present tense), the **prefix separates** and moves to the **end of the sentence**. The base verb is conjugated normally and stays in position 2 [citation:2][citation:6].

### Example:
- **anrufen** (to call) → Ich **rufe** meine Mutter **an**. (I call my mother.)

| Base Verb | Prefix | Separable Verb | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| rufen (to call) | an- | **anrufen** | to call (on phone) |
| stehen (to stand) | auf- | **aufstehen** | to get up |
| kaufen (to buy) | ein- | **einkaufen** | to shop |
| kommen (to come) | mit- | **mitkommen** | to come along |
| machen (to do/make) | auf- | **aufmachen** | to open |
| machen (to do/make) | zu- | **zumachen** | to close |

## How to Use Separable Verbs

### 1. Present Tense (Main Clause) ✅ SEPARATE
In main clauses, the **prefix goes to the end** [citation:2][citation:6][citation:10].

| Verb | Sentence | Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **aufstehen** | Ich **stehe** um 7 Uhr **auf**. | I get up at 7 o'clock. |
| **anrufen** | Er **ruft** seine Mutter **an**. | He calls his mother. |
| **einkaufen** | Wir **kaufen** im Supermarkt **ein**. | We shop at the supermarket. |
| **mitkommen** | **Kommst** du **mit**? | Are you coming along? |
| **aufmachen** | **Mach** bitte die Tür **auf**! | Please open the door! (Imperative) |

### 2. With Modal Verbs (Infinitive) ❌ NO SEPARATION
When used with modal verbs (können, müssen, wollen), the separable verb stays together as an infinitive at the end [citation:7][citation:10].

| Sentence | Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| Ich **muss** um 7 Uhr **aufstehen**. | I have to get up at 7 o'clock. |
| Er **will** seine Mutter **anrufen**. | He wants to call his mother. |
| Wir **möchten** heute **einkaufen**. | We would like to shop today. |

### 3. Perfect Tense (Past) - The "ge-" Goes in the Middle
In the perfect tense, the **-ge-** of the past participle is inserted **between the prefix and the base verb** [citation:4][citation:7][citation:10].

| Verb | Past Participle | Sentence | Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| aufstehen | **aufgestanden** | Ich bin früh **aufgestanden**. | I got up early. |
| anrufen | **angerufen** | Hast du ihn **angerufen**? | Did you call him? |
| einkaufen | **eingekauft** | Wir haben **eingekauft**. | We shopped. |
| mitkommen | **mitgekommen** | Bist du **mitgekommen**? | Did you come along? |

## Important: Separable vs. Inseparable Verbs

Not all verbs with prefixes are separable! Some prefixes are **always inseparable** [citation:4][citation:6][citation:10]:

### Inseparable Prefixes (never separate)
- **be-**, **emp-**, **ent-**, **er-**, **ge-**, **miss-**, **ver-**, **zer-**

| Verb | Meaning | Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **besuchen** | to visit | Ich **besuche** meine Oma. (NOT: Ich suche meine Oma besuchen ❌) |
| **verstehen** | to understand | Er **versteht** die Frage. |
| **verkaufen** | to sell | Wir **verkaufen** unser Auto. |

### Compare: Separable vs. Inseparable
| Separable | Inseparable |
| :--- | :--- |
| **ein**kaufen (to shop) → Ich kaufe ein. | ver**kaufen** (to sell) → Ich verkaufe. |
| **an**rufen (to call) → Ich rufe an. | be**rufen** (to occupy) → Ich berufe. |
| **auf**stehen (to get up) → Ich stehe auf. | ver**stehen** (to understand) → Ich verstehe. |

**Pronunciation clue:** If the **stress** is on the **prefix**, it's separable. If the stress is on the **base verb**, it's inseparable [citation:4][citation:10].

## Summary: When to Separate?

| Situation | Separated? | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Present tense (main clause) | ✅ YES | Ich **rufe** dich **an**. |
| Imperative (command) | ✅ YES | **Ruf** mich **an**! |
| With modal verbs (infinitive) | ❌ NO | Ich möchte dich **anrufen**. |
| In subordinate clauses (with "dass", "weil") | ❌ NO | ...weil ich dich **anrufe**. [citation:5][citation:10] |
| Perfect tense (past) | ⚠️ ge- in middle | Ich habe dich **angerufen**. |
