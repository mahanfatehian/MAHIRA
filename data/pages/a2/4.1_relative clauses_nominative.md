# Relative clauses: nominative - A2

A relative clause describes a noun. If the relative pronoun is the subject, it matches the gender of that noun, and the verb goes to the end.

## Why this matters
- Job, person, and object descriptions need this clause
- **der / die / das** here is not the article again. It is a pronoun
- The verb never stays in position 2 inside the clause

## The pronoun as subject

| Noun | Pronoun | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| der Beruf | **der** | Das ist der Beruf, **der** zu mir passt. |
| die Firma | **die** | Die Firma, **die** mich eingeladen hat, ist klein. |
| das Büro | **das** | Das Büro, **das** hell ist, gefällt mir. |
| die Menschen (plural) | **die** | Menschen, **die** gern im Team arbeiten, passen hierher. |

The pronoun takes the gender of the noun it replaces, not the gender of the person in real life.
