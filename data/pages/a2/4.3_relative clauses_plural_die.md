# Relative clauses: plural die - A2

Plural nouns, whatever their singular gender, take **die** in both the nominative and the accusative relative clause.

## Why this matters
- *Menschen*, *Kollegen*, *Bücher* do not keep *der* or *das*
- A plural subject and a plural object look the same: **die**
- You still have to put the verb at the end

## One pronoun, two jobs

| Role | Example |
| :--- | :--- |
| Subject | Das sind die Kollegen, **die** mir helfen. |
| Object | Das sind die Bücher, **die** ich gelesen habe. |

Compare the singular:

| Singular | Plural |
| :--- | :--- |
| der Kollege, **der** hilft | die Kollegen, **die** helfen |
| der Roman, **den** ich lese | die Romane, **die** ich lese |
