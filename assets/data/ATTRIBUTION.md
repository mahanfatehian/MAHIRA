# Verb conjugation data — attribution

`german_verbs.sqlite` contains the principal parts of ~8,000 German verbs
(present ich/du/er, Präteritum, Partizip II, Konjunktiv II, the imperatives and
the auxiliary). MAHIRA expands these into full six-person paradigms at runtime
(see `src/core/conjugation.py`).

**Source:** the data was extracted from **German Wiktionary** (de.wiktionary.org)
by the open *german-verbs-database* project
(https://github.com/viorelsfetea/german-verbs-database).
