## Official level ranges

Hueber publishes Menschen at A1, A2, and B1; Starten wir! at A1, A2, and B1;
and Sicher! as a separate B1+, B2, and C1 series. There is no official Menschen
B2/C1/C2, Starten wir! B2, or Sicher! C2 cover, so no artwork is fabricated or
mislabelled for those names.

- [Menschen series](https://www.hueber.de/reihe/menschen/)
- [Starten wir! series](https://www.hueber.de/reihe/starten-wir)
- [Sicher! series](https://www.hueber.de/reihe/sicher/)
