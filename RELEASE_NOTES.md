## MAHIRA 0.2.0

Offline-first German study — now with smarter answer-checking, in-card audio, and two new study tools.

### Highlights

- **Smarter answer matching** — vocabulary meanings are checked *semantically* with an offline model, so valid paraphrases ("to work" / "work") are accepted instead of marked wrong.
- **In-card audio with adjustable speed** — hear the word, the example, or the sentence at **slow / normal / fast** on the vocabulary and listening cards.
- **New — Vocab Table.** A study grid of a Lektion's vocabulary (Vocab · Article · Plural · Meaning · Type). Hide any column from the toolbar *or* by clicking its header, then tap a blanked cell to reveal just that entry — so you can self-quiz column by column.
- **New — Verb Conjugation.** Tap a verb in the Vocab Table to open its full paradigm across **Präsens, Präteritum, Perfekt, Plusquamperfekt, Futur I, Konjunktiv II** and the **Imperativ**. Completely offline, expanded from a bundled ~8,000-verb dataset.
- **Study ergonomics** — keyboard ratings (1–4), one-step undo (Ctrl+Z), focus mode (F11), next-interval previews on the rating buttons, and a "due now" nudge on the launch screen.
- **More A1 content** — additional Menschen and Starten Wir Lektionen.

### Downloads

- **Windows** — the installer (`.exe`) or the portable `.zip`.
- **macOS (Apple Silicon)** — the `.dmg` or `.zip`. The app is ad-hoc signed; if Gatekeeper blocks it on first launch, right-click → **Open**.

Everything runs locally — no account, and no internet required after install.
