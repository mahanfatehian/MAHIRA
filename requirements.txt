# numpy is pinned to the 1.26.x line on purpose. numpy 2.x ships a per-process

# PyInstaller that extension can be registered under two module paths
# (numpy._core vs the legacy numpy.core shim) and startup dies with
# "ImportError: cannot load module more than once per process". 1.26.4 has no
# such split/guard and is accepted by every other pin here (scipy needs
